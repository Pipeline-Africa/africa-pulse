## Administrative Context

Officially, "Johannesburg" is the **City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality**, a Category A metropolitan municipality under the [Local Government: Municipal Structures Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/local-government-municipal-structures-act) ([municipal profile](https://municipalities.co.za/metropolitans/view/2/City-of-Johannesburg-Metropolitan-Municipality)). It is not a province or a group of local government areas. Category A metros sit outside South Africa's district municipalities, so no district council sits between the metro and the province ([Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/local-government-municipal-structures-act)).

The name is used for several different-sized areas:

### 1. City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality (official local government)
- **Status:** Category A metropolitan municipality; the legal "city"
- **Area:** 1,642.6 km²
- **Population:** 4,803,262 (2022 census: [Stats SA Census 2022](https://census.statssa.gov.za/))
- **Internal divisions:** Seven administrative regions (A–G) since 2006, replacing eleven numbered regions created in 2000. Regions have no separate councils ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg)).
- **Identifiers:** OSM relation 594508; Wikidata Q2346838

### 2. Johannesburg "main place" (historic city)
- **Status:** Census statistical unit, not a separate government
- **History:** Established 1886; city status 1928
- **Area:** 334.81 km²
- **Population:** 957,441 (2011 census)
- **Identifiers:** Wikidata Q34647; OSM node 261833893

### 3. Wider urban area
- **Status:** No administrative status
- **Population:** Wider-area estimates range from 7,860,781 (2011) to about 15 million (2022); estimates use different geographies and are not directly comparable. The 2022 figure is also the Gauteng Province census population ([Johannesburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg); [Stats SA Census 2022](https://census.statssa.gov.za/)).

### Larger unit
Johannesburg is in **Gauteng Province**, one of South Africa's nine provinces, and is Gauteng's provincial capital.

### Hierarchy
Republic of South Africa → Gauteng Province → City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality → Regions A–G → Wards

## Coverage notes

### 1. Census 2022 undercount
- Reported undercount of 31%, about 10 percentage points above the previous highest (Comoros, 2017). ([Daily Maverick, 10 July 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-07-10-south-africas-2022-census-unfit-for-purpose-demographers-explain-what-went-wrong.md))
- UCT demographers (Moultrie and Dorrington) advise using the data with extreme caution. ([Daily Maverick, 10 July 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-07-10-south-africas-2022-census-unfit-for-purpose-demographers-explain-what-went-wrong.md))
- Stats SA says an expert review under the Statistics Council validated the results. ([Business Day, 11 July 2024](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/national/2024-07-11-stats-sa-defends-the-validity-of-its-census/))
- In August 2024, Stats SA announced that certain census data would not be released. ([News24, 26 August 2024](https://www.news24.com/business/opinion/moultrie-and-dorrington-rerunning-census-is-pointless-but-sa-still-needs-alternative-estimates-20240826))

### 2. Hard-to-count housing
- Census officials had limited access to gated estates and suburban homes. ([TimesLive, 18 October 2023](https://www.timeslive.co.za/ideas/2023-10-18-opinion-south-africas-2022-census-has-johannesburg-stopped-growing-or-are-the-numbers-wrong/))
- Wikipedia cites 29% of residents in informal dwellings (2001 census data, now dated). ([Wikipedia, "Johannesburg"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg))
- Gang-occupied ("hijacked") buildings in Hillbrow are unlikely to appear reliably in census, address or utility data. ([Wikipedia, "Johannesburg"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg))

### 3. Boundary changes
- The old city merged with surrounding townships and settlements into the metropolitan municipality (2000). ([Wikipedia, "Johannesburg"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg))
- Region-level data before and after 2006 do not align, because the 11 regions of 2000 were reorganized into 7 (A–G). ([Wikipedia, "Johannesburg"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg))
- Record which Municipal Demarcation Board boundary version each dataset uses; the 2018 release has 205 local and 8 metropolitan municipalities. ([MDB metadata via CSIR GIS service](https://pta-gis-2-web1.csir.co.za/server2/rest/services/Temp_group_roads_2040_60_webmap_MIL1/MapServer/18?f=pjson))
- Area differs slightly between sources: 1,642.6 km² ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg)) vs 1,644 km² ([Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q34647)).

### 4. Weather station outside the city
- Standard climate normals (1991–2020) come from O. R. Tambo International Airport, in neighboring Ekurhuleni, at 1,695 m. ([Wikipedia, "Johannesburg" climate table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg))
- The airport is about 58 m lower than the city's reference elevation of 1,753 m. ([Wikipedia, "Johannesburg"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg))

### 5. Air-quality monitoring gaps
- The city reported operating 8 stations in 2019, with crime and vandalism interrupting operation. ([Mail & Guardian via PressReader, 28 June 2019](https://www.pressreader.com/south-africa/mail-guardian/20190628/281659666582505))
- October 2025: 15 of 31 Gauteng stations functional, per the Gauteng Environment Department ([EWN, 16 October 2025](https://ewn.co.za/2025/10/16/gauteng-environmental-dept-concerned-over-air-monitoring-controls-in-the-province)); 12 of 31 fully operational, per Daily Maverick ([Daily Maverick, 22 January 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-01-22-environmental-watchdogs-say-rotten-egg-s-smell-over-joburg-sign-of-air-pollution/)).
- Equipment cannot run during load shedding. ([South African Journal of Science](https://sajs.co.za/article/view/16009/20313))
- aqicn.org temporarily disabled the Orange Farm station's AQI in September 2025 because readings were implausibly low. ([aqicn.org, Orange Farm](https://aqicn.org/city/south-africa/johannesburg-metro/orange-farm/km/m))
- Gauteng plans to roll out 124 low-cost sensors. ([EWN, 16 October 2025](https://ewn.co.za/2025/10/16/gauteng-environmental-dept-concerned-over-air-monitoring-controls-in-the-province))
