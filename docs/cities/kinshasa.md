# Kinshasa

## Canonical name

Kinshasa. This is the English label used by [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3838) and the name of the [GeoNames capital record](https://www.geonames.org/2314302).

## Country

Democratic Republic of the Congo (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: `CD`). [GeoNames lists the record in CD](https://www.geonames.org/2314302), and [Wikidata identifies the country as the Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3838).

## Aliases and local names

`Kinsásá` is a Lingala name recorded by [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/2314302). `Léopoldville` (also `Leopoldville` and `Leopoldstad`) is the former name; [Wikidata dates the French official name Léopoldville to 1881–1966](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3838).

## Coordinates

-4.32171, 15.31225 (WGS 84), the representative OpenStreetMap place point for Kinshasa ([node 27043346](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/27043346)). The nearby [Wikidata coordinate](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3838) is 4°19′19″S, 15°18′43″E; the small difference reflects separate source geometries.

## Time zone

`Africa/Kinshasa` (UTC+01:00; no daylight-saving time). It is the time-zone name on the [GeoNames record](https://www.geonames.org/2314302); the [IANA Time Zone Database source for Africa](https://github.com/eggert/tz/blob/main/africa) records the identifier and its offset rules.

## Administrative context

Kinshasa is the national capital and a first-level administrative division (a city-province), not merely a municipality within another province; [Wikidata describes it as a city with province status](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3838). The city-province has 24 communes: the [DRC government's UGPTN profile](https://www.ugptn.cd/fr/project/provinces/kinshasa) gives that total, and the [UNDP city profile](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/migration/cd/UNDP-CD-Profil-Ville-Kinshasa.pdf) lists the communes, including Nsele and Maluku.

## Competing definitions

- **City-province (official administrative boundary):** the DRC government project's Kinshasa profile reports **14,565,700 people (2020 estimate)** across **9,965 km²** and 24 communes. This is the broad legal city-province definition. [UGPTN](https://www.ugptn.cd/fr/project/provinces/kinshasa)
- **Urban agglomeration:** the UN's *World Urbanization Prospects 2025* reports **13,211,000 people (2025)** for Kinshasa's urban agglomeration. This statistical urban-settlement definition is not the same thing as the city-province's legal boundary. [UN DESA, *World Urbanization Prospects 2025*](https://desapublications.un.org/file/21497/download)

These figures should not be substituted for one another: they cover different definitions and reference years. The distinction is especially important because the legal city-province contains extensive non-urban land.

## Coverage notes

Treat the city-province boundary and the built-up city as separate coverage scopes. Kinshasa's official 9,965 km² area includes the eastern, largely rural communes of Maluku and Nsele; a [geographic study of the city boundary](https://journals.openedition.org/belgeo/pdf/15421) describes them as the two large rural communes, while the [UNDP profile](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/migration/cd/UNDP-CD-Profil-Ville-Kinshasa.pdf) confirms they are part of the 24-commune province. Point-based or urban-only sources may therefore omit material parts of the official city-province.
