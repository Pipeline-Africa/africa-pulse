
# Nairobi

> Reference metadata for Nairobi, Kenya. The canonical city record and the wider metropolitan planning region are distinct geographic concepts.

## Canonical name

Nairobi.

Nairobi is the capital and largest city of Kenya. Use `Nairobi` as the canonical English name for the city.

Source: [Wikidata – Nairobi (Q3870)](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3870).

## Administrative context

Nairobi is the capital of Kenya and is administered as Nairobi City County.

Kenya's devolved system of government recognises 47 counties. Nairobi City County is one of these counties and has its own county government.

The county is geographically and administratively distinct from the neighbouring counties of Kiambu, Machakos and Kajiado.

Nairobi City County began operating under the devolved government structure on 4 March 2013, following the 2010 Constitution and the 2013 transition to county governments.

The distinction between Nairobi city and Nairobi City County is important:

- Nairobi city is the urban settlement and capital.
- Nairobi City County is the official administrative jurisdiction.
- Nairobi Metropolitan Area is a wider regional planning concept covering multiple counties.

Sources:
- [Kenya National Bureau of Statistics – 2019 Census, Volume I](https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/2019-Kenya-population-and-Housing-Census-Volume-1-Population-By-County-And-Sub-County.pdf).

## Competing definitions

Nairobi's population and geographical extent depend on which administrative or urban definition is being used.

### 1. Nairobi City County

The 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census recorded:

- Population: 4,397,073
- Land area: 703.9 km²
- Population density: 6,247 people/km²
- Census year: 2019.

This figure represents the population within Nairobi City's administrative county boundary.

Source: [KNBS – 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census, Volume I, Table 2.4](https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/2019-Kenya-population-and-Housing-Census-Volume-1-Population-By-County-And-Sub-County.pdf).

### 2. Nairobi Metropolitan Area

The Nairobi Metropolitan Area (NMA), as used in the NaMATA regional planning context, covers five counties:

| County | 2019 population |
|---|---:|
| Nairobi | 4,397,073 |
| Kiambu | 2,417,735 |
| Machakos | 1,421,932 |
| Kajiado | 1,117,840 |
| Murang'a | 1,056,640 |
| **Total** | **10,411,220** |

The metropolitan definition covers a wider regional population than Nairobi City County alone.

The five-county metropolitan region includes residential and economic activity extending beyond Nairobi's administrative boundary.

Source: [JICA – Transport Situation in Nairobi City (2021), Nairobi Metropolitan Area section](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12342721_02.pdf).

### Implications

The two population figures should not be treated as interchangeable.

- Use 4,397,073 when reporting Nairobi City County's 2019 population.
- Use 10,411,220 when reporting the five-county Nairobi Metropolitan Area's 2019 population.
- Do not assign metropolitan population figures to the Nairobi City County boundary.
- Preserve the geographic definition and census/reference year alongside population observations.

## Coverage notes

- Nairobi City County and Nairobi Metropolitan Area have different geographic extents.
- Weather stations and other monitoring locations should be assigned to the appropriate geographic boundary.
- Data collected from neighbouring counties should not automatically be classified as Nairobi City County data.
- Population and other statistical datasets should retain their reference year and geographic definition.
- GeoNames, Wikidata and OpenStreetMap identifiers represent different geographic objects and should not be treated as interchangeable.
- The metropolitan region's five-county definition should be explicitly identified whenever used.
