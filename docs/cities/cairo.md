# Cairo

## Canonical name

Cairo. [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q85) uses this English label for Egypt's capital, and [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/360630/cairo.html) records it as a capital feature.

## Country

Egypt (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: `EG`). [GeoNames places Cairo in EG](https://www.geonames.org/360630/cairo.html), and [Wikidata identifies Egypt as its country](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q85).

## Aliases and local names

The Arabic name is `القاهرة` (*al-Qāhirah*, also transliterated *Al Qahirah* or *El Qahira*). [Wikidata records the Arabic official and native label](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q85) and English aliases including *Qahira* and *Cairo, Egypt*; transliterations should not be treated as distinct cities.

## Coordinates

30.04439, 31.23573 (WGS 84), the representative point of the OpenStreetMap city relation for Cairo ([relation 5466227](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5466227)). GeoNames gives a different representative point, 30.06263, 31.24967, for its Cairo capital record; the difference reflects separate source geometries. [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/360630/cairo.html)

## Time zone

`Africa/Cairo`. Egypt uses UTC+02:00 outside daylight-saving time and UTC+03:00 during daylight-saving time; the [IANA Time Zone Database source for Africa](https://github.com/eggert/tz/blob/main/africa) defines the identifier and its rule changes. GeoNames also assigns its Cairo record to [`Africa/Cairo`](https://www.geonames.org/360630/cairo.html).

## Administrative context

Cairo is Egypt's capital and lies in Cairo Governorate; it is not simply a municipality inside another governorate. [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q85) identifies Cairo as the national capital and as located in Cairo Governorate. The governorate's own portal says Cairo City is organized into four areas and 38 districts. [Cairo Governorate Portal](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/about-governorate/cairo-in-lines/)

"Greater Cairo" is a wider, semi-official metropolitan term: the Cairo Governorate Portal describes it as including Giza Governorate and some of its suburbs, plus Shubra El-Kheima in Qalyubia Governorate. It therefore crosses governorate boundaries and has no single Cairo Governorate administrative boundary. [Cairo Governorate Portal](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/about-governorate/cairo-in-lines/)

## Competing definitions

- **Cairo Governorate / Cairo City:** CAPMAS reports **9,570,400 people (2017)** in Cairo Governorate, whose total area is **3,085.12 km²**. This is the narrow official governorate definition. [CAPMAS population-density table](https://www.capmas.gov.eg/Admin/News/PressRelease/201991215340_Migration%20Booklet%20Eng.pdf)
- **Greater Cairo:** Cairo Metro reports **20,901,000 people (2020 estimate)** for Greater Cairo. The Cairo Governorate Portal's definition shows why that exceeds the governorate: it includes territory in Giza and Qalyubia as well as Cairo. [Cairo Metro](https://www.cairometro.gov.eg/en/about/1), [Cairo Governorate Portal](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/about-governorate/cairo-in-lines/)
- **UN statistical city:** the UN's *World Urbanization Prospects 2025* estimates **25,600,000 people (2025)** for Al-Qahirah (Cairo). This is a statistical city/urban-settlement estimate, not a legal Cairo Governorate boundary; it should not be substituted for either of the administrative definitions above. [UN DESA](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/sites/www.un.org.development.desa.pd/files/undesa_pd_2025_wup2025_summary_of_results.pdf)

## Coverage notes

Choose the boundary to match the data source. Governorate-level data covers Cairo Governorate only, while metropolitan transport, urban-area, and market sources may use Greater Cairo or another statistical-city footprint. Because Greater Cairo includes parts of Giza and Qalyubia, a source labelled only "Cairo" needs its geographic scope checked before aggregation. [Cairo Governorate Portal](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/about-governorate/cairo-in-lines/)
